# N3XT

> Your instant USD payment network for B2B transactions. Full-reserve banking with programmable, 24/7/365 real-time transaction settlement on blockchain rails.

Core capabilities and key features:
- **Full-Reserve Architecture**: Narrow bank structure operating under a Special Purpose Depository Institution (SPDI) charter. N3XT does not lend customer deposits; all funds are backed 1:1 by cash or short-term U.S. Treasuries, eliminating duration risk and traditional fractional-reserve credit risks.
- **Tokenized Dollar Deposits**:  Operates on a blockchain based core banking platform that enables the movement of real US dollars on blockchain payment rails.
- **N3XT Digital Dollar**: The N3XT Digital Dollar (NDD) is a native, bank-regulated tokenized asset backed 1:1 with US dollars and short term U.S. Treasuries. It combining public blockchain speed and programmability with the regulatory safety of a standard bank deposit. 
- **Closed-Loop Security**: NDD transactions require all counterparties to be fully KYC/KYB verified, holding an on-chain "Identity Passport" (NDDID NFT) to mitigate compliance and fraud risks.
- **Programmable Payment Logic**: Employs smart contracts and API integrations to let treasury managers, stablecoin issuers, and global logistics enterprises automate payments instantly when pre-defined and coded conditions are met.
- **Enterprise Ecosystem Integration**: Removes banking portal friction through native integrations with trusted digital asset custodians including Fireblocks and Utila.
- **Developer Friendly**: API integrations and MCP availability allow customers to take actions within their existing treasury and AI ecosystem..
